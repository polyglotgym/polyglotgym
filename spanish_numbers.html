How to Use This App

What this is

A drill for Spanish number words. Spanish numbers up to a trillion are compositional — you can build any number once you know a handful of base forms and patterns. This app drills the composition, with a strong focus on the traps that trip learners up.

The deck is roughly 110 cards, covering 0–20, the 21-29 family, round tens, hundreds with their irregular forms, thousands, millions, and a set of representative composites and years.

Learn the patterns, then drill the traps.

The two directions
  • Number → Words (default) — you see the numeral and type the Spanish words. This is production.
  • Words → Number — you see the Spanish words and type the numeral. This is comprehension, and it's a genuinely different skill.

Both directions drill the same underlying cards. Progress is shared — a card you know in Production is also Known in Comprehension.

The four piles
⚪ ActiveCards you're still working through. Appear in every drill. A card is Active if it's new or if you've failed it once and corrected it.
🟡 PendingCards you've recently missed. Mixed into the general rotation but called out separately. Pin with 📍 or use 📌 All.
🔴 PinnedYour watchlist. Stays in rotation indefinitely. Auto-pin after 3 fails (adjustable).
💚 KnownMastered. Skipped in Adaptive mode. To review, switch to Full Sweep or Forget All.
Card Sets
Four drill modes
  • All cards — the full deck, including tricky traps and years.
  • Standard only — excludes tricky traps and years. The "plain" drill.
  • Tricky only — just the trap cards (veintiuno, quinientos, cien mil, etc). 20 cards or so.
  • Years only — 16 historical years, each with a trivia fact shown in feedback.
The Rules
0–15: irregular base

Every number up to 15 is memorised as a single word. cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once, doce, trece, catorce, quince.

16–19: dieci- compounds

16 = dieciséis, 17 = diecisiete, 18 = dieciocho, 19 = diecinueve. Note the accent on dieciséis.

20–29: veinti- family

21-29 are written as one word starting with veinti-: veintiuno, veintidós, veintitrés, veinticuatro, veinticinco, veintiséis, veintisiete, veintiocho, veintinueve. Traps: veintidós, veintitrés, veintiséis all carry accents on the final vowel.

30–99: tens + y + units

Round tens are single words: treinta, cuarenta, cincuenta, sesenta, setenta, ochenta, noventa. Composite tens use y: treinta y tres, cuarenta y dos.

100 vs 101+: cien vs ciento

Standalone 100 is cien. But anything over 100 uses ciento: ciento uno, ciento veinte, ciento cuarenta y siete. This is one of the most common traps.

200–999: hundreds with three traps

Most hundreds are just [unit] + -cientos (doscientos, trescientos, cuatrocientos, seiscientos, ochocientos). Three are irregular: quinientos (500, not "cincocientos"), setecientos (700, not "sietecientos"), novecientos (900, not "nuevecientos").

1000 and up

1000 is mil — not "un mil". 2000 is dos mil. Any thousands count that ends in "uno" truncates: 21,000 = veintiún mil (not "veintiuno mil"); 101,000 = ciento un mil.

Millions and billions

1,000,000 = un millón — "un", not "uno". 2,000,000 = dos millones (plural). 21,000,000 = veintiún millones. 1,000,000,000 = mil millones (literally "a thousand millions" — Spanish doesn't use "billón" for 10⁹; billón is 10¹²).

Context-Dependent Forms

This app drills the standalone forms. Two truncations apply only when a number precedes a noun, and are not drilled here:

  • uno → un before masculine singular noun: un libro (not "uno libro"). Before feminine: una mesa.
  • veintiuno → veintiún / veintiuna: veintiún años (masculine), veintiuna sillas (feminine).

These are context-dependent and belong to a sentence-context drill. This app drills standalone forms.

Suggested Workflow

Start with Standard only. Get the plain patterns automatic — 0–20, tens, hundreds — before touching the traps.

Then Tricky only. 20 cards. Run these as a dedicated session. This is where you internalise veintiuno, quinientos, cien vs ciento, veintiún mil.

Then Years only. Optional. Years are essentially 4-digit numbers with trivia attached — worth doing once to see them in context, but not a distinct skill.

Switch direction. Once you can produce the words, drill Comprehension. Listening comprehension is where the number really clicks — you'll start hearing "mil quinientos treinta" in the wild and recognise it instantly.

The Traps

veintiuno, not veinte y uno. 21–29 are single words.
cien standalone, ciento in compounds. 100 = cien; 101+ = ciento.
quinientos, setecientos, novecientos. 500, 700, 900 are irregular.
mil, not un mil. 1,000 is just mil.
un millón, not uno millón. 10⁶ truncates uno → un.
veintiún mil, not veintiuno mil. 21,000 truncates.
cien mil, not ciento mil. 100,000 keeps cien.

Context-Dependent Forms

uno → un before masc. sg. noun (un libro); uno → una before fem. sg. noun (una mesa).
veintiuno → veintiún / veintiuna before noun (veintiún años, veintiuna sillas).
These are not drilled — this app drills the standalone forms.
