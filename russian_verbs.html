How to Use This App

What this is

A high-intensity drill tool for one thing: Russian verb conjugation — verbs, aspects, and the tense/mutation patterns that trip up learners.

Verb conjugation and aspect are the central problems in Russian. If you can't conjugate verbs quickly, your speech will falter. If you can't choose between imperfective and perfective, you'll never be properly understood — Russian speakers will know something is wrong even if the words are technically correct.

This app drills the 12 core aspect pairs (24 verbs) across three tenses and six pronouns, with careful handling of the past-tense gender distinction, consonant mutations, and the perfective/imperfective future split.

Think of this app as like a metronome for verb conjugation.

The four piles

The Reviews tab shows four piles. Each one has a purpose:

⚪ ActiveCards you're still working through. Appear in every drill. A card is Active if it's new or if you've failed it once and corrected it.
🟡 PendingCards you've recently missed. Mixed into the general rotation but called out separately so you can drill them deliberately. The Drill Pending button exists for this. Pin individually with 📍, or use 📌 All to pin every pending card at once.
🔴 PinnedYour watchlist. Stays in rotation indefinitely and never auto-clears. Auto-pin kicks in after 3 total fails (adjustable). To retire one, correct it, then unpin.
💚 KnownMastered. Skipped in Adaptive mode. To see it again, switch to Full Sweep, or Forget All.
How cards move between piles

Cards change state based on your first answer in each session:

FromCorrect (first attempt)Wrong

If you fail a card and then get it right later in the same session, that later correct doesn't count for state changes — it's just practice. The state already moved when you failed.

Russian-Specific Behaviour
Aspect (НСВ / СВ)

Every verb in this deck is paired with its aspectual counterpart — читатьchitat' / прочитатьprochitat', писатьpisat' / написатьnapisat', etc. The imperfective describes ongoing or repeated action; the perfective describes completed action.

The two aspect filters in the drawer let you drill only imperfectives, only perfectives, or both. The verb pair is shown in the prompt so you always see the counterpart.

Tense behaviour by aspect

This is the crucial difference from Romance languages:

  • Imperfective has all three tenses: present (читаюchitayu), past (читалchital), and a compound future (буду читатьbudu chitat').
  • Perfective has no present tense. What would be "present endings" are actually the simple future (прочитаюprochitayu). Its past is straightforward (прочиталprochital).
Past-tense gender

Past-tense forms agree with the subject's gender/number, not person. Я читал (masculine speaker) vs Я читала (feminine speaker). The Speaker Gender toggle in the drawer controls which form you're drilling. For ты and он/она, both forms are generated because both are possible. The app accepts either gender for ты.

Cyrillic vs transliteration

Input accepts either. Typing читаешь or chitaesh' or just the ending -ешь are all correct. Apostrophes for soft signs (') are optional. ё is normalised to е automatically.

The Script Display settings in the drill drawer control how the verb identifier appears in prompts. If you can't yet read Cyrillic, toggle Cyrillic off. If you don't want translit cluttering the prompt, toggle Transliteration off. The answer feedback always shows both scripts regardless of these settings, so you're always reinforcing the association.

The Settings
Adaptive vs Full Sweep

Adaptive (default) draws from Active + Pending + Pinned, skips Known. This is how you grind through the deck.

Full Sweep draws from everything, including Known. Useful for a check-up. Failing a Known card in Sweep demotes it back to Pending.

Production vs acoustic
  • Production Lane — you see the English gloss and type the Russian form.
  • Acoustic Lane — you hear the Russian form and identify person, aspect, and tense.

Progress tracking doesn't distinguish between the two. One mental form, one card. Play Mode lets you test without recording anything.

Drilling
Reading the scoreboard
  • Correct / Checked — lifetime totals.
  • Streak — resets on any wrong answer; 👑 means 15+.
  • Known: X / Y — under current filters. Narrow the filters and both numbers shrink.
  • Progress: X / Y — current session only.
Hotkeys

Space or Enter for move on / correct, X (or 1) for incorrect, 2 for correct (honour mode), Ctrl+Z to undo the last answer.

Suggested Workflow

Beginners. Start with imperfective only (uncheck Perfective in the drawer) and present tense. Learn the endings. Once present is comfortable, add past, then future.

Upper beginners. Introduce perfective verbs — but keep in mind they have no present tense. Their "future" uses present-like endings on the perfective stem.

Intermediate. Open the whole deck. Drill the pair you're studying together — читать and прочитать, писать and написать. The pair helps you internalise the aspect distinction.

Every third or fourth session. Hit Drill Pending in the Reviews tab. This is where you patch the holes.

When you're tired. Turn on 🎮 Play Mode. Nothing gets recorded.

Final Note

This deck covers 12 high-frequency aspect pairs across present, past, and future. Not covered: participles, gerunds, the imperative, or the conditional/subjunctive (бы + past). Those are separate topics. The 12 pairs here are the backbone — master these first and every other verb you meet will slot into place.

Notes

Hush consonants (ш, ж, ч, щsh, zh, ch, shch) take -у/-ут-u/-ut instead of -ю/-ют-yu/-yut. E.g. пишу, пишутpishu, pishut.
Stress-shifted ё in forms like берёшьberyosh', даёшьdayosh'. Typing with or without ё both accepted.
Perfective present: perfective verbs have no present tense — the "fut" forms shown are the simple future.
Imperfective future: compound — forms of бытьbyt' + infinitive (буду читатьbudu chitat').
