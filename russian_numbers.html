Declines like a hard adjective. The app drills masculine forms.

2 — feminine form две

Nominative два is masculine/neuter; feminine is две. All other cases are the same across genders (двух, двум, двумя, двух). Accusative is animacy-dependent: два / две (inanimate) vs двух (animate).

3, 4 — same pattern as 2

No feminine variant in nominative. Accusative is animacy-dependent: три / четыре (inanimate) vs трёх / четырёх (animate).

Animate accusative

Numeral accusative is animacy-dependent. With inanimate nouns (books, tables) it matches nominative. With animate nouns (people, animals) it matches genitive. This app drills the inanimate accusative — the form you'll see most often in everyday reading.

ё / е in numerals

The forms трёх, трём, трёмя, четырёх, четырём, четырьмя contain ё. Because many writers omit ё in practice, the app treats ё and е as interchangeable — трех and трёх both pass. In formal writing, use ё.

Traps, at a glance

40 / 90 / 100 — only two forms each.

50–80 — both halves decline; instrumental in -ьюдесятью.

200–400 — irregular stems in gen (двухсот, трёхсот, четырёхсот).

500–900 — instrumental in -ьюстами.

2 — nominative feminine is две.
